If a lake page shows a level, full-pool value, date or name that looks wrong, email {{site.corrections_email}}. We reply to every report within 24 hours.

What to include

  • the lake and the page address;
  • what the page shows, and the date and time printed with it;
  • what you believe is correct, and where you saw it;
  • a link to the agency or operator page that shows the right figure, if you have one.

What happens next

  1. We compare the page with the source agency's own data for the same date and time.
  2. If our page differs from the source, we fix the cause (for example, a wrong gauge, a wrong datum or a stale feed) and tell you what changed.
  3. If the source itself looks wrong, we can hold the lake as "Current level unknown", with the reason shown, until the source is fixed. We never replace a reading with a number typed by hand.
  4. If the page matches the source, we reply to explain the difference, such as a daily average compared with an instant reading, or two agencies using different datums.

Fixed errors are noted in the page's "About this reading" block for as long as they matter.

Do not wait for a correction before acting. For anything that affects your plans or safety, use the operator's own information and follow local officials.
