  • No accounts and no sign-up forms. You do not need to give any personal information to use the site. There are no email alerts or newsletters at this time.
  • Lake search runs in your browser from a list on this site. What you type is not sent to us.

  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages and features are used (for example, searches, downloads and clicks to source agencies). It uses Google's Consent Mode v2: advertising storage is turned off everywhere, and in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland analytics cookies are off by default. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Google's privacy policy applies to that data.
  • Your settings. Small preferences you choose on the site, such as a unit or chart setting, may be kept in your own browser's storage. They are not sent to us.
  • Email. If you email us, we use your message only to reply and to fix the site, and we do not share it.
  • Advertising. The site does not show ads today. Before that changes, this page will be updated to name the ad provider and its consent tool.
  • Children. The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
