  • The data is provisional. Agencies publish many lake readings before they have been reviewed, and they can revise or remove them later. Gauges can fail or report wrong values for a time. We pass on what the source publishes, with its date; we cannot guarantee that it is correct or complete.
  • Readings are dated, and some are daily averages. A daily average describes a whole day, not this moment. A level can change quickly during a flood or a large release. Always read the date and time printed with each level.
  • Not for navigation, flood or safety decisions. A lake level does not tell you the depth at a ramp, dock or channel, or whether a hazard is present. Do not use this site to decide whether to boat, swim, launch, cross water or stay near a dam or river.
  • In an emergency, follow local officials and the National Weather Service. During floods, heavy rain or dam releases, take instructions from your local emergency managers, the dam operator and the National Weather Service. Call 911 if you are in danger.
  • Closures, ramp status and lake rules are set by the lake's operator and park managers. Check their pages, which each lake page links.
