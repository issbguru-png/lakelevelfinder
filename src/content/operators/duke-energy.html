Duke Energy operates hydroelectric lakes in the Carolinas, including the lakes of the Catawba-Wateree system, and posts their levels on its own website.

We have not confirmed a public data feed from Duke Energy that we can use, so we do not copy levels from its pages. A Duke Energy lake shows a level on this site only where a public USGS gauge we can use measures it. Duke notes that its reported levels are measured at its hydro station dams. For current levels and lake information, use Duke Energy's lakes pages.

Sources: Duke Energy: lakes and recreation · Duke Energy: dam and lake level basics.
