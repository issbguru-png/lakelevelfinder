Appalachian Power, an American Electric Power (AEP) company, operates the Smith Mountain Project in Virginia, which includes Smith Mountain Lake and Leesville Lake, and posts their levels on the project's website.

We have not confirmed a public data feed from AEP that we can use, so we do not copy levels from its pages. These lakes show a level on this site only where a public USGS gauge we can use measures them. For current levels and why they change, use the Smith Mountain Project's lake levels page.
