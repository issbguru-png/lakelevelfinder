The Bureau of Reclamation, part of the U.S. Department of the Interior, runs major western water projects. They include the Central Valley Project in California, with 20 dams and reservoirs and Shasta Dam as its keystone, and Glen Canyon Dam, which forms Lake Powell, the second-largest reservoir in the United States. Reclamation also reports the daily data for Hoover Dam and Lake Mead, the largest.

Reclamation publishes its water data in RISE, its system for viewing and downloading Reclamation water data, and in regional reports such as the Lower Colorado River daily report. Reclamation states that RISE data "are provisional and subject to revision unless otherwise noted." Our pages read Reclamation's daily series and show each reading with its own date. RISE does not carry full-pool values, so those come from Reclamation pages, linked on each lake page.
