The Grand River Dam Authority (GRDA) is Oklahoma's largest public power electric utility. It is funded by electricity and water sales rather than tax money, manages more than 70,000 surface acres of lake water, and posts current levels for Grand Lake, Lake Hudson and Holway on its website.

GRDA's lake levels page does not describe a download or data feed, and we have not found a public feed we can use, so we do not copy levels from its pages. GRDA lakes show a level on this site only where a public gauge we can use measures them. For current levels and river operations, use GRDA's own pages.
