The Texas Water Development Board (TWDB) does not operate dams. Its Water Data for Texas site gathers lake levels from gauges run by partners, including USGS, the International Boundary and Water Commission, the Corps of Engineers, the Bureau of Reclamation and LCRA, and turns them into estimated daily averages, storage and percent full for the state's monitored reservoirs. TWDB polls the gauge data about every six hours and publishes only the daily average. It converts levels to storage with elevation-area-capacity curves from its hydrographic surveys.

TWDB's percent full is conservation storage divided by conservation capacity, with flood pool and dead pool storage left out, so it stops at 100% when a lake is above full pool. TWDB describes its figures as best estimates and gives no warranties. Our Texas pages (and Elephant Butte in New Mexico) read TWDB's daily data, credit TWDB, and link each lake's TWDB page.
