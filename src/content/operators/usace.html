The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers operates flood-control and multipurpose reservoirs through regional districts, such as the Little Rock, Tulsa, Fort Worth, Mobile and Savannah districts. On a Corps flood-control lake, the top of the conservation pool is the normal or target operating level. Water above it is flood storage, held to reduce flooding downstream and released later under the lake's water control plan.

Corps districts publish pool elevations through the Corps Water Management System (CWMS) data service, for example hourly or every 15 minutes. What each district publishes, and how its series are named, differs from district to district. Our Corps lake pages use a district's elevation series only after we have matched it to the lake and checked it against another public figure; each page names the district and the series, and prints the datum the Corps records for the project.

Sources: U.S. Army: When the Water Rises · USACE CWMS Data API · CWMS: district offices · USACE HEC: reservoir operations basics.
