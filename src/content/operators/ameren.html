Ameren Missouri operates Bagnell Dam, which forms Lake of the Ozarks, under a license from the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, and posts lake level reports on its own website.

We have not confirmed a public data feed from Ameren that we can use, so we do not copy levels from its pages. Lake of the Ozarks shows a level on this site only where a public USGS gauge we can use measures it. For current levels, forecasts and the lake's operating rules, use Ameren's pages.

Sources: Ameren: lake level reports · Ameren Missouri: lake fluctuation policy.
