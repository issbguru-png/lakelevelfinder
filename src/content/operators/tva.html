The Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) operates the dams and reservoirs of the Tennessee River system and posts lake levels for them on its own website.

We have not confirmed a public data feed from TVA that we can use, so we do not copy levels from TVA's pages. A TVA lake shows a level on this site only where a public USGS or Corps gauge we can use measures it. For current levels, planned drawdowns and operations on TVA lakes, use TVA's lake levels page.

Source: TVA: lake levels.
