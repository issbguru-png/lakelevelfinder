The Lower Colorado River Authority (LCRA) operates six dams on the lower Colorado River in Central Texas, which form the six Highland Lakes: Buchanan, Inks, LBJ, Marble Falls, Travis and Austin. Lake Buchanan and Lake Travis are the region's water supply reservoirs. Lake Travis is the only Highland Lake designed to hold back floodwater, and LCRA operates it during floods under U.S. Army Corps of Engineers regulations.

LCRA posts current lake levels, a daily river operations report and flood operations information on its Hydromet site. Our Highland Lakes pages read the Texas Water Development Board's daily data, which takes its levels for Buchanan, Inks, LBJ, Travis and Austin from LCRA. For flood operations, floodgate notices and lake conditions, use LCRA's own pages.
