Several of Georgia's large lakes are Corps of Engineers projects. The Mobile District's records include Buford Dam and Lake Lanier, Allatoona Dam and Lake, Carters Dam and Lake, and West Point Dam and Lake. The Savannah District's records include Hartwell Dam and Richard B. Russell Dam in Georgia and J. Strom Thurmond Dam in South Carolina, on the upper Savannah River.

The levels on our Georgia pages come from the Corps Water Management System data service, where districts publish pool elevations hourly or every 15 minutes. Some reservoirs follow a seasonal guide curve, a target level that changes through the year, so a level below summer full pool in winter can be the operator's target. Lakes run by utilities get a level only where a public gauge we can use measures them; otherwise the page links to the operator.
