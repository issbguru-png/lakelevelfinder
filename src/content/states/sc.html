On South Carolina's border with Georgia, the Corps of Engineers' Savannah District runs three large lakes on the upper Savannah River: Hartwell, Richard B. Russell and J. Strom Thurmond.

The levels on our South Carolina pages come from the Corps Water Management System data service, where the district publishes pool elevations, and from USGS gauges where they measure a lake. Lakes run by utilities get a level only where a public gauge we can use measures them; otherwise the page links to the operator. Some reservoirs follow a seasonal guide curve, a target level that changes through the year, so a level below summer full pool in winter can be the operator's target.

Sources: CWMS: Savannah District projects · USACE Savannah District: upper Savannah River reservoirs · USGS Water Data APIs · USACE HEC: guide curves.
