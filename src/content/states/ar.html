Arkansas's large reservoirs are mostly Corps of Engineers projects run by the Little Rock District. Its records include Bull Shoals and Beaver dams on the White River, Norfork Dam on the North Fork River, Greers Ferry Dam on the Little Red River, and Millwood, DeQueen, Dierks, Gillham, Nimrod and Blue Mountain dams, along with the locks and dams of the Arkansas River.

The levels on our Arkansas pages come from the Corps Water Management System data service, where the district publishes pool elevations, for example hourly at Table Rock Dam. Full pool comes from the district's published top of conservation pool where it is available. Each reading shows its own time and the Corps series it came from.

Sources: CWMS: Little Rock District projects · USACE CWMS Data API.
