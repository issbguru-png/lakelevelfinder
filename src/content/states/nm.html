Elephant Butte Reservoir is on the Rio Grande about midway between Albuquerque and El Paso. It is the main storage reservoir of the Bureau of Reclamation's Rio Grande Project. In the north, Navajo Reservoir on the San Juan River is a Reclamation storage project, and the Corps of Engineers' Albuquerque District records lakes such as Abiquiu, Cochiti, Conchas and Santa Rosa.

The Elephant Butte level on our pages comes from the Texas Water Development Board's Water Data for Texas, which includes the lake with data provided by Reclamation and publishes a daily average, percent full and storage. Other New Mexico lakes use the Corps Water Management System data service or Reclamation data where a usable series exists.
