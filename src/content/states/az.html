Arizona shares the main Colorado River reservoirs, and the Bureau of Reclamation reports data for all of them. Lake Powell is formed by Glen Canyon Dam in Arizona, and Glen Canyon National Recreation Area stretches from Lees Ferry in Arizona into southern Utah. Downstream, Lake Mead and Lake Mohave lie in Lake Mead National Recreation Area, which covers parts of Arizona and Nevada. Lake Havasu, formed by Parker Dam, is the lake from which the Metropolitan Water District of Southern California and the Central Arizona Project pump Colorado River water.

Reclamation publishes daily elevations for Lake Mead, Lake Mohave and Lake Havasu in its Lower Colorado River daily report and in its RISE data system, and Glen Canyon Dam operations data through its Upper Colorado Basin region. Reclamation describes its RISE data as provisional. Our pages read these Reclamation sources and show each reading with its own date.
