Nevada's best-known reservoir, Lake Mead, is formed by Hoover Dam on the Colorado River and is the largest reservoir in the United States. Lake Mead National Recreation Area covers parts of Nevada and Arizona and includes both Lake Mead and Lake Mohave, which was created by Davis Dam downstream. Reclamation reports daily releases and elevations for Hoover and Davis dams.

Our Nevada pages read Reclamation's own data. Reclamation's RISE data system carries a daily Lake Mead elevation series that is updated once a day, usually about one day behind, and Reclamation states that the data "are provisional and subject to revision unless otherwise noted." Its Lower Colorado River daily report also lists elevations for Lake Mead, Lake Mohave and Lake Havasu in feet above mean sea level. RISE does not carry a full-pool value, so full pool on our pages comes from a Reclamation page, linked on each lake page.
