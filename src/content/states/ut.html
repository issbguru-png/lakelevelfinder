Lake Powell, behind the Bureau of Reclamation's Glen Canyon Dam in Arizona, is the second-largest reservoir in the United States. Glen Canyon National Recreation Area stretches from Lees Ferry in Arizona to the Orange Cliffs of southern Utah. Flaming Gorge Dam on the Green River releases water that helps keep Lake Powell above critical elevations.

Reclamation's Upper Colorado Basin region publishes operations data for Glen Canyon and Flaming Gorge dams and reservoir data through its HydroData pages. Our Utah pages read Reclamation data and show each reading with its own date. The National Park Service explains how changing Lake Powell levels affect visits.
