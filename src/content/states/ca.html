California's largest reservoirs belong to two big water projects. The Bureau of Reclamation operates the federal Central Valley Project, whose keystone is Shasta Dam; Shasta Lake, on the Sacramento River about nine miles northwest of Redding, is the largest reservoir in California. Reclamation's Central California Area Office is based at Folsom Dam, east of Sacramento. Lake Oroville is part of the State Water Project, run by the California Department of Water Resources (DWR).

The levels on our California pages come from the California Data Exchange Center (CDEC), which DWR manages. CDEC publishes daily, and for some stations hourly, reservoir elevation and storage by station. The newest rows are sometimes blank until they are filled in, so a page may show yesterday's or an earlier reading with its own date. CDEC does not supply a capacity figure in the data we read, so percent full on our pages is storage divided by a capacity published by DWR or Reclamation, with that source linked.
