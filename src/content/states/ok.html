Most of Oklahoma's large lakes are Corps of Engineers projects in the Tulsa District, whose records include Eufaula, Keystone, Oologah, Kaw, Tenkiller Ferry, Fort Gibson, Skiatook, Broken Bow and Hugo lakes, and Lake Texoma on the Red River at the Texas line. The Grand River Dam Authority (GRDA), Oklahoma's largest public power utility, manages more than 70,000 surface acres of lake water and posts levels for Grand Lake, Lake Hudson and Holway on its own website.

The levels on our Oklahoma pages come from the Corps Water Management System data service where the district publishes a pool elevation series. We have not found a public data feed from GRDA that we can use, so GRDA lakes show a level here only if a public gauge we can use measures them; otherwise the page links to GRDA.

Sources: CWMS: Tulsa District projects · GRDA · GRDA: lake levels.
