Several of Missouri's large lakes are Corps of Engineers projects. The Little Rock District's records include Table Rock Dam on the White River and Clearwater Dam on the Black River, both in Missouri. The St. Louis District's records include Mark Twain Lake on the Salt River and Wappapello Lake. Lake of the Ozarks, behind Bagnell Dam, is operated by Ameren Missouri, which posts lake levels on its own website.

The levels on our Missouri pages come from the Corps Water Management System data service, where districts publish pool elevations hourly or every 15 minutes, or from USGS gauges where they measure a lake. We have not confirmed a public data feed from Ameren that we can use, so Lake of the Ozarks shows a level here only if a public gauge we can use measures it; otherwise the page links to Ameren.
