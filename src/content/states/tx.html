Texas reservoirs are run by many different operators. The Lower Colorado River Authority operates the six Highland Lakes on the Colorado River, including Lake Buchanan and Lake Travis. Corps of Engineers districts run flood-control and water-supply lakes such as Canyon Lake and Lewisville Lake (Fort Worth District) and Lake Texoma (Tulsa District). The International Boundary and Water Commission is the legal repository of data for Amistad and Falcon reservoirs on the Rio Grande.

The levels on our Texas pages come from the Texas Water Development Board's Water Data for Texas, which collects gauge data from USGS, the Boundary and Water Commission, the Corps of Engineers, the Bureau of Reclamation and LCRA and publishes an estimated daily average for each monitored reservoir, usually for the previous day. TWDB also publishes percent full, which is based on storage: conservation storage divided by conservation capacity, with flood pool storage left out. For lakes shared with Mexico or a neighboring state, such as Amistad, Falcon, Texoma and Toledo Bend, TWDB's figures use the Texas share. Each lake has its own vertical datum, printed on its page.
