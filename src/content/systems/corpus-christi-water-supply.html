The City of Corpus Christi draws its water from two directions. To the west, Choke Canyon Reservoir and Lake Corpus Christi in the Nueces River Basin are the city's longest-established and largest supplies. To the east, the Mary Rhodes Pipeline brings water from Lake Texana (Phase I) and from the Colorado River (Phase II). The Texas Water Development Board groups Choke Canyon, Lake Corpus Christi and Lake Texana as Corpus Christi's monitored water supply reservoirs.

The city uses the combined capacity of Choke Canyon Reservoir and Lake Corpus Christi to trigger its drought stages, as a 2001 Agreed Order with the State requires.
