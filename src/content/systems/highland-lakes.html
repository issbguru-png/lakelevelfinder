The Highland Lakes are six lakes on the lower Colorado River in Central Texas, formed by six dams that the Lower Colorado River Authority (LCRA) operates: Lake Buchanan (Buchanan Dam), Inks Lake (Inks Dam), Lake LBJ (Wirtz Dam), Lake Marble Falls (Starcke Dam), Lake Travis (Mansfield Dam) and Lake Austin (Tom Miller Dam). LCRA describes Lake Buchanan and Lake Travis as the region's water supply reservoirs, serving more than 1 million people as well as businesses, industry, agriculture and the environment. Lake Travis is the only Highland Lake designed to hold back floodwater; LCRA operates it during floods under U.S. Army Corps of Engineers regulations.

Because Buchanan and Travis hold the water supply, LCRA reports their combined storage alongside its daily lake levels. The combined figure on this page adds the two lakes' storage from the Texas Water Development Board's daily data, which takes its Highland Lakes levels from LCRA. LCRA posts its own current lake levels and a daily river operations report on its Hydromet site.
